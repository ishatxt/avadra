About the shelves

About the collection

One shelf, a quiet reader, and a house that keeps its mouth shut.

AVADRA is an independent, local-first reading room. It gathers the collection into one reader that remembers your place. It is not affiliated with any publisher, estate, or rights holder.

The editions open directly in the reader, with typography and layout original to AVADRA. Harry Potter and related names belong to their respective rights holders.

Progress, bookmarks, and settings stay in this browser. Local PDFs you add stay here too and are never uploaded. Bring a copy you have the right to read.

i

One reader, three moods

Midnight, Parchment, and Dark. Adjustable reading width, zoom, fullscreen, bookmarks, thumbnails, and in-book search, all in one quiet reader.

ii

Nothing leaves the browser

Progress, bookmarks, and preferences live in local storage. Personal PDFs live in IndexedDB. There is no account, no analytics, and no upload.

iii

Built to be light

No framework, no CDN, no build step. PDF.js is served locally, images are lazy-loaded, and the homepage is a handful of small files. It is meant to run on modest machines.

About this shelf

A private shelf, not an official edition.

The Wizarding Library is an unofficial, personal catalogue of the seven Harry Potter novels. It is not affiliated with J. K. Rowling, Bloomsbury, Scholastic, Warner Bros., or any other rights holder.

The interface, catalogue records, and reader are original to this project. The PDFs and cover images are personal copies supplied by the reader. They are not offered for download and should not be redistributed.

i

Reading order, kept straight

Every volume is numbered from one to seven and dated by its original publication year. The default sort follows the story, not the alphabet.

ii

Private copies, plainly marked

Each record points to a PDF in the books/ folder and a cover in assets/covers/. If either file is missing, the page says so instead of failing silently.

iii

Your place, kept quietly

Reading position, bookmarks, and display preferences are stored in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing site data clears your place; there is no account to recover.
